## asr/vocab.txt — word list (SCOWL / Hunspell en_US)

`asr/vocab.txt` is a lowercase list of English words, generated by expanding the affix rules of the Hunspell `en_US` dictionary (Debian package `hunspell-en-us` 1:2020.12.07, based on SCOWL). It is a modified version of that word list (affixes expanded, all words lowercased), used only to decide whether a recognized word is a known English word.
