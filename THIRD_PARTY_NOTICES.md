## asr/vocab.txt — word list (SCOWL / Hunspell en_US)

`asr/vocab.txt` is a lowercase list of English words, generated by expanding the affix rules of the Hunspell `en_US` dictionary (Debian package `hunspell-en-us`, based on SCOWL). It is used only to decide whether a recognized word is a known English word.

SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists) is maintained by Kevin Atkinson. Source: http://wordlist.sourceforge.net/
