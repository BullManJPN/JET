Okay, let me share my screen. This is the evaluation harness that our team uses to compare different versions of Claude. We feed in a few hundred prompts, we collect the responses, and then a grader model scores each one. The context window is long enough that we can include the whole transcript, which helps with multi step tasks.
One thing we learned is that retrieval augmented generation, or RAG, is not always the right answer. If the documents fit in the context window, just put them in. If not, then you need an embedding model and a vector store, and you have to think about chunking, and latency, and cost per token.
