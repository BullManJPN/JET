So, for the Mac app, we are using Swift and the Foundation Models framework. The on device model is fast, but it is small, so for harder translation we send the text to a larger model through Private Cloud Compute. For speech, SpeechAnalyzer gives us partial results about once a second, and we confirm a word once two updates agree on it.
Then there is the question of vocabulary. Words like Claude, Anthropic, Hugging Face, Ollama, and Kubernetes are not common in everyday speech, so the recognizer often gets them wrong. We tried giving it a list of contextual strings, and we also tried fine tuning a language model on our own transcripts.
